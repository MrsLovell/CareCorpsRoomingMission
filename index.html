Meet Nurse Nova

The Room Raider has mixed up the clinic. Nurse Nova needs your help to get each patient ready for their visit. At work, this is called rooming.

This is a practice game. You’ll get the room ready, check the patient’s name and date of birth, and check vital signs like pulse and blood pressure.

Pick or make a hero. Then pick a skill on the Mission Board. Play the stages in order.

  1. Watch. Read each step with Nurse Nova.
  2. Try With Help. Put the steps in order. Ask for a hint if you get stuck.
  3. Safety Check. Spot the step that could cause harm.
  4. Do It on Your Own. Put the steps in order without hints.
  5. Become a Legend. Pick a rule you would teach a new hero.
